# HF-ARQ Modem Smoke 0.2.0-alpha.7

Executable: `HF-ARQ-Modem-Smoke.exe`, SHA-256
`94D030E5E4A0163C42A47E6B7BFA1A0063FD932796CD9186E231305867383DB3` (also in
`SHA256SUMS.txt`). P1 continues with this executable from the repeat block of Mercury.

Maintenance release during Phase 1 (P1). The channel simulator, the client's measurement,
the success rule and the counting are the same as in 0.2.0-alpha.5 and alpha.6; results
of all three versions are judged identically (each run records its version).

## Why

On 2026-11-15, after the Mercury campaign block, the cross-check with the other PC's ZIP
did not finish on Windows. Each results ZIP also carried the other PC's results unpacked
by earlier cross-checks, so copies were nested inside copies, and unpacking the new ZIP
produced paths longer than Windows' 260-character limit. The repeat block then read the
previous (pre-campaign) cross-check and found nothing to repeat.

## What changes

- **Results ZIPs** (Create results ZIP and the ZIP at the end of a block) leave out the
  other PC's results unpacked by cross-checks (`cross_checks/*/other_pc`); the cross-check
  reports themselves are kept. ZIPs are also much smaller.
- **Cross-check** does not unpack the other PC's own cross-checks (also for ZIPs made by
  earlier versions) and uses extended-length paths on Windows.
- **Repeat block** uses only the most recent cross-check: if it did not finish, or has no
  Phase 1 attempts of the modem in use, the block does not start and says why.
