# HF-ARQ Modem Smoke 0.2.0-alpha.5

Pre-release of the bench with the **Phase 1 channel simulator** (protocol v5.3). It
adds a simulated HF channel between the two PCs and runs the measurements in blocks.
It contains no campaign results yet.

## Validated on real hardware (2026-11-12)

Two Windows PCs, three Behringer UCA222 (the third, UCA-S, on PC B for the simulator),
four single-conductor RCA connections, BW500, callsigns XQ2CG-1 / XQ2CG-2. Mercury
1.9.19 (official Windows ZIP) and VARA HF 4.9.1 (the same binary on both PCs).

- **CAL through the simulator** (Transparent): A->B SNR 53.0 dB, SINAD 52.6 dB;
  B->A SNR 63.7 dB, SINAD 60.4 dB; verdict ok on both paths.
- **9-test sequence through the simulator** (Transparent), 9 / 9 pairs for each arm,
  with each arm's RMS power reference saved:

| Arm | Drive | RMS A->B | RMS B->A | Highest peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mercury 1.9.19 | — | -13.04 dBFS | -12.85 dBFS | -6.5 dBFS |
| VARA HF unlicensed | -3 dB | -10.55 dBFS | -11.83 dBFS | below -3 dBFS |
| VARA HF licensed | -7 dB | -14.55 dBFS | -15.81 dBFS | -5.65 dBFS |

- **Minimum replication kit** (Moderate: 1 ms, 0.5 Hz, +3 dB RMS SNR; 200 B; 10
  attempts; every simulator attempt valid, no dropouts):

| Arm | Successes | 95 % Wilson | Median delivery | Median effective SNR (A->B) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VARA HF licensed | 10 / 10 | 72–100 % | 46.9 s | 2.88 dB |
| Mercury 1.9.19 | 10 / 10 | 72–100 % | 56.3 s | 3.05 dB |
| VARA HF unlicensed | 10 / 10 | 72–100 % | 66.1 s | 2.94 dB |

The kit checks that a bench reproduces the method; it is not the campaign.

## What is new

- **Channel simulator** (PC B, Tools > Channel simulator): two-path Watterson model with
  the ITU-R F.1487 delays and spreads (Good, Moderate, Poor), Gaussian noise in 3 kHz,
  seeds published per condition, direction and attempt, output 12 dB below the input,
  dropout and clipping checks.
- **SNR referred to each arm's RMS power** (primary), measured once per arm in its
  Transparent sequence and never adjusted during a transfer; -3 dBFS peak ceiling; the
  PEP-limited profile is kept as secondary. Each attempt records its effective SNR.
- **Phase 1 cells** with the same-session ACK and a deadline (10 min for 200 B, 60 min
  for 10 KiB); the cross-check matches each attempt with the simulator's and gives,
  per cell, the success rate with a 95 % Wilson interval, the median delivery time and
  the median effective SNR (`phase1_cells.csv`). Kit, pilot and campaign have separate
  series (KIT, PILOT, P1).
- **Blocks**: one RUN per PC runs a whole stage for the modem in use (pilot, campaign,
  or repeat of the missing and not-counted attempts). The simulator in Automatic mode
  takes each attempt's condition, number and arm from PC B. A block resumes by itself,
  stops after three attempts in a row that point to the bench, refuses to start if the
  modem binary or VARA's Drive changed, and ends with a named results ZIP.
- **Phase 1 success rule as defined**: `counted_success` = B verified the payload's
  SHA-256 and A verified B's ACK (same run and hash) before the deadline, with no
  infrastructure or procedure fault. A missing BUFFER 0 from the modem is a warning; the
  raw `sender_completed` is kept as recorded. Cells report their successes without
  BUFFER 0. A verification series (`VERIFY-poor-200B-01…10`) checks the rule on the bench.
- **Modem versions**: any version, but the same binary on both PCs and throughout the
  campaign; the cross-check rejects pairs from different binaries.
