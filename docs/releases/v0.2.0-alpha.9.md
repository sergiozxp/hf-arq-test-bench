# HF-ARQ Modem Smoke 0.2.0-alpha.9

Executable: `HF-ARQ-Modem-Smoke.exe`, SHA-256
`28D5BF1BD7AD79E506A06C384D176523EC47274A42544CC410D468267DD436A5` (also in
`SHA256SUMS.txt`). The VARA HF unlicensed campaign resumes with this executable from
P1-moderate-10KiB-10 (plan position 100).

Correction to alpha.8's rule that keeps PC A and PC B in step. The success rule, the
channel and the payload are unchanged.

## Why

On 2026-11-18, during the VARA HF unlicensed campaign block, PC B stopped listening for an
attempt before PC A had called (P1-good-10KiB-16 and -20, P1-moderate-10KiB-07, -09 and
-10). VARA's two ends report the end of a lost session up to a minute apart: PC A's modem
was still closing the previous session when PC B announced the next attempt, the simulator
took that audio as PC A's call, and after 60 s of silence PC B concluded that PC A had
stopped calling. Four times PC A's real call then reached PC B while its run was closing;
the fifth time (P1-moderate-10KiB-10) PC B moved on and was one attempt ahead.

## What changes

- **Simulator (Automatic mode):** an attempt starts only with audio from PC A after at least
  30 s of silence, so the tail of PC A's previous session is not taken as its call. (PC A
  always waits 105 s before calling.)
- **PC B without a session** sends LISTEN OFF first, before its cleanup DISCONNECT, so that a
  call cannot connect while the run winds down.
- **Cross-check:** a pair in which PC B gave up listening but PC A's call then connected is
  not counted (the PCs were out of step).
