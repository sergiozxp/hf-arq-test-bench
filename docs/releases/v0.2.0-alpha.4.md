# HF-ARQ Modem Smoke 0.2.0-alpha.4

Pre-release of the two-PC smoke bench (profile MS-1.0, protocol v5.2). With this
version the smoke profile is **complete**: it adds the same-session ACK (E0-3) and a
calibration of the bench with its own test signal. It is **not** a performance
measurement.

## Validated on real hardware (2026-10-23)

Two Windows PCs, one Behringer UCA222 each, direct audio cable (no channel
simulator), BW500, callsigns XQ2CG-1 / XQ2CG-2. Each arm ran the full sequence of
9 tests (E0-1 16 B, 1 KiB, 10 KiB; E0-2 V1 and V2; E0-3; H1 x3), one RUN per PC,
and was cross-checked pair by pair:

| Arm | Pairs passed | E0-3 ACK round trip |
|---|---|---|
| Mercury 1.9.19 (official Windows ZIP) | 9 / 9 | 31.5 s |
| VARA HF 4.9.0, licensed (`LINK REGISTERED`) | 9 / 9 | 19.7 s |
| VARA HF 4.9.0, unlicensed (`LINK UNREGISTERED`) | 9 / 9 | 47.0 s |

Bench calibration (clean cable, left channel): A->B SNR 74.1 dB, SINAD 71.2 dB;
B->A SNR 77.5 dB, SINAD 73.8 dB; no clipping; the two sound cards differ by 12.3 ppm.

## What is new

- E0-3: B writes `ACK run=<12-hex> SHA256=<16-hex>` in the same open session and A
  verifies it before disconnecting; the cross-check requires it.
- Bench calibration ("CAL" test, modems stopped): SNR, SINAD, level, clipping and
  clock offset of both audio paths, and the highest usable simulated SNR.
- Full sequence of 9 tests; the sender waits 60 s more after E0-3 (Mercury reports
  the end of that session to PC B about 40 s later).
- The session report lists stopped runs and the latest calibration of each path.
